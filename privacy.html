Spectralux — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-04

Spectralux does not collect, store, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking.

Microphone

Spectralux uses the microphone solely to analyze the beat and frequencies of music playing near your device in real time, so the visuals can react. Audio is processed live on your device and is never recorded, stored, or transmitted anywhere.

Song identification

If you use song identification, Spectralux uses Apple's ShazamKit, which sends an anonymous audio fingerprint (not the audio itself) to Apple to recognize the track. This is handled by Apple and governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Lyrics

If a future version's lyrics feature is enabled, Spectralux would look up lyrics by song title and artist from a third-party lyrics service. No personal information is sent.

Local network (Apple TV)

When casting to an Apple TV running Spectralux, the app sends a small stream of visual/beat data to that device over your local network. This data stays on your network and is not collected by us.

Children

Spectralux contains no objectionable content and collects no data, making it suitable for all ages.

Spectralux — Support

Spectralux turns any music in the room into a real-time GPU light show.

Getting started

  1. Open Spectralux and tap Start, then allow microphone access.
  2. Play music out loud nearby — from any speaker, record player, or venue.
  3. The visuals lock to the beat after a few seconds. Tap the screen to show controls; swipe the picker to change visualizers; tap the sliders button for sensitivity, speed, color, brightness, and auto-shuffle.

Cast to Apple TV

Install Spectralux on an Apple TV on the same Wi-Fi and turn on Cast to Apple TV in settings. Your iPhone senses the music and the Apple TV renders the show in sync.

Troubleshooting

  • No reaction? Make sure music is audible to the phone and the mic permission is granted (Settings → Spectralux → Microphone).
  • Visuals lag or lead the beat? Adjust the Sync offset slider in settings.
  • Apple TV not connecting? Confirm both devices are on the same Wi-Fi network and Local Network access is allowed.
